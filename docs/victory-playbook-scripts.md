# Victory Playbook - All Story Scripts

## Story 1: The Busy Executive
**Objection:** "I'm actually really busy right now."
**Phase:** Opening
**Panels:** 11

| # | Speaker | Dialogue |
|---|---------|----------|
| 1 | Narrator | A sunny afternoon in a nice suburban neighborhood. A GeckoGreen rep walks up a well-maintained driveway. |
| 2 | Rep | Hi there! I'm with GeckoGreen—we're treating lawns in your neighborhood today. |
| 3 | Homeowner | I'm actually really busy right now. |
| 4 | Narrator | The rep doesn't push back—instead, they acknowledge the pressure. |
| 5 | Rep | I totally get it—you're busy. This will take less than 60 seconds, I promise. |
| 6 | Homeowner | Okay, 60 seconds... |
| 7 | Rep | Quick question—who handles your lawn care right now? |
| 8 | Homeowner | I do it myself, mostly. |
| 9 | Rep | I can tell you put effort into it. I noticed a few spots that might need professional treatment—can I show you what I mean? |
| 10 | Homeowner | Sure, show me. |
| 11 | Narrator | By respecting their time and asking a qualifying question, the rep earned the right to continue. |

## Story 7: The Skeptical Analyst
**Objection:** "I need to do my own research first."
**Phase:** Full Sale (Knock to Close)
**Panels:** 31

| # | Speaker | Dialogue |
|---|---------|----------|
| 1 | Narrator | A sunny Saturday morning. Marcus approaches a well-maintained colonial home with a lawn that has some visible issues. |
| 2 | Rep | Good morning! I'm Marcus with GeckoGreen. We're treating several lawns on your street this week. |
| 3 | Homeowner | Hi. I'm David. What exactly do you do? |
| 4 | Rep | We specialize in professional lawn treatment - fertilization, weed control, and pest prevention. I noticed your lawn has some concerning spots. Mind if I take a quick look? |
| 5 | Homeowner | I need to do my own research first before I commit to anything. |
| 6 | Rep | I respect that, David. You seem like someone who makes informed decisions. What kind of information would help you decide? |
| 7 | Homeowner | I want to know what's actually in the products you use. And compare prices. |
| 8 | Rep | Smart approach. I can walk you through exactly what we apply and why. Would a 5-minute lawn assessment be helpful for your research? |
| 9 | Homeowner | I suppose that would give me something to compare against. |
| 10 | Narrator | David leads Marcus to the backyard where several problem areas are visible. |
| 11 | Rep | See these lighter patches here? That's iron chlorosis - your soil pH is likely too high. And this spreading patch is creeping charlie. |
| 12 | Homeowner | I've tried treating the creeping charlie myself. It keeps coming back. |
| 13 | Rep | Because most retail products only kill what's visible. The root system runs underground. Our treatment includes a systemic that kills the whole plant network. |
| 14 | Homeowner | What's in your systemic treatment? |
| 15 | Rep | The active ingredient is triclopyr. It targets broadleaf weeds without harming grass. I can email you the full product specs if you'd like. |
| 16 | Homeowner | That would actually be helpful. |
| 17 | Rep | I'll send that today. Now, this brown patch here - you might be dealing with grubs. Mind if I check? |
| 18 | Narrator | Marcus pulls back a section of turf, revealing grub larvae. |
| 19 | Homeowner | Wow, I had no idea those were in there. |
| 20 | Rep | Most people don't until the damage is severe. By then, you're looking at major reseeding costs. |
| 21 | Homeowner | What does your program include for grubs? |
| 22 | Rep | We do preventive grub treatment in early summer. It costs $45 as an add-on, but saves you hundreds in lawn restoration. |
| 23 | Homeowner | What about the basic program cost? |
| 24 | Rep | For your lawn size, it's $69 per application, 8 applications per year. That covers fertilization, pre and post-emergent weed control, and spot treatments. |
| 25 | Homeowner | How does that compare to TruGreen or other companies? |
| 26 | Rep | We're competitive with national brands but we're locally owned. That means if there's ever an issue, you call me directly. Not a call center. |
| 27 | Homeowner | And if I'm not satisfied? |
| 28 | Rep | We retreat for free. If we can't fix it after multiple attempts, full refund. No questions asked. |
| 29 | Homeowner | Alright, you've given me good information. I'm still going to research a bit, but... how do I move forward if I decide to go with you? |
| 30 | Rep | I can set you up for our early spring treatment today. If you prepay the annual program, you save 10%. And you can still cancel if your research turns up something concerning. |
| 31 | Narrator | By respecting David's analytical nature and providing detailed, verifiable information, Marcus earned both trust and the sale. |

## Story 8: The Reluctant Decision-Maker
**Objection:** "I need to talk to my husband first."
**Phase:** Full Sale (Knock to Close)
**Panels:** 33

| # | Speaker | Dialogue |
|---|---------|----------|
| 1 | Narrator | A weekday afternoon. Marcus approaches a nice suburban home where a woman is gardening in the front flower bed. |
| 2 | Rep | Good afternoon! Beautiful petunias you've got there. I'm Marcus with GeckoGreen. |
| 3 | Homeowner | Thank you! I'm Jennifer. What can I help you with? |
| 4 | Rep | We're treating several lawns in the neighborhood. I noticed you've got some dandelion pressure starting up. Mind if I take a quick look? |
| 5 | Homeowner | I guess that would be okay. But I should tell you - I need to talk to my husband before making any decisions. |
| 6 | Rep | Absolutely, that makes total sense. What I can do is give you all the information so you two can discuss it together. Fair enough? |
| 7 | Homeowner | That sounds reasonable. |
| 8 | Narrator | Marcus walks the lawn with Jennifer, pointing out various issues. |
| 9 | Rep | See how the grass is thinner here near the driveway? That's heat stress combined with compacted soil. |
| 10 | Homeowner | We've tried to grow grass there for years. Nothing works. |
| 11 | Rep | It needs aeration and the right seed blend. Most retail seed isn't designed for high-stress areas. |
| 12 | Homeowner | That makes sense. What else do you see? |
| 13 | Rep | Your crabgrass is about two weeks from exploding. Without a pre-emergent now, you'll be fighting it all summer. |
| 14 | Homeowner | My husband usually handles the lawn, but he's been so busy with work. |
| 15 | Rep | That's actually why a lot of couples go with professional treatment. It takes the pressure off. What does your husband typically do for the lawn? |
| 16 | Homeowner | He buys stuff from Home Depot and tries to keep up. But honestly, it never looks as good as some of our neighbors. |
| 17 | Rep | Would it help if I could show you exactly what we'd do and the expected results? Then you'd have specifics to share with him. |
| 18 | Homeowner | Yes, that would make the conversation easier. |
| 19 | Rep | Our program runs $65 per treatment, 8 times a year. We handle everything - timing, products, application. You just enjoy the results. |
| 20 | Homeowner | What about the bare spot by the driveway? |
| 21 | Rep | We can include overseeding in the fall treatment. That area needs shade-tolerant fescue, not what you'd find at a big box store. |
| 22 | Homeowner | How much extra is that? |
| 23 | Rep | The fall seeding is included if you sign up for the annual program. It's $520 for the year, but you get all 8 treatments plus seeding. |
| 24 | Homeowner | That seems reasonable... but I really do need to run it by Mike first. |
| 25 | Rep | I understand completely. What if I left you with a written quote? That way you have something concrete to show him. |
| 26 | Homeowner | That would be helpful. |
| 27 | Rep | I can also do one better - if you give me his number, I'm happy to call him directly and answer any questions. Would that help? |
| 28 | Homeowner | Actually... he's in his home office right now. Let me see if he can step out. |
| 29 | Narrator | Jennifer goes inside and returns with her husband Mike. |
| 30 | Homeowner | This is Mike. I was telling him about the crabgrass situation. |
| 31 | Rep | Mike, good to meet you. Your wife gave me a great tour. The lawn has solid bones - it just needs the right treatment program. |
| 32 | Homeowner | Jennifer said something about $520 for the year? That's less than I spend on products I don't even know how to use properly. |
| 33 | Narrator | By respecting Jennifer's decision process and providing clear information to share, Marcus turned one decision-maker into two - and closed the sale. |

## Story 9: The DIY Warrior
**Objection:** "I can do it myself for way less."
**Phase:** Full Sale (Knock to Close)
**Panels:** 38

| # | Speaker | Dialogue |
|---|---------|----------|
| 1 | Narrator | A Saturday morning. Marcus notices a homeowner actively spreading fertilizer with a push spreader. |
| 2 | Rep | Morning! I see you're a fellow lawn enthusiast. Mind if I ask what you're putting down? |
| 3 | Homeowner | Just some fertilizer from Lowe's. I do all my own lawn care. |
| 4 | Rep | That's awesome. I'm Marcus with GeckoGreen. What brand are you using? |
| 5 | Homeowner | Scott's Turf Builder. It works fine. I can do it myself for way less than what you guys charge. |
| 6 | Rep | I respect the DIY approach. What's your annual routine look like? |
| 7 | Homeowner | Fertilizer in spring and fall, weed killer when I see weeds, I've got it handled. |
| 8 | Rep | Nice. Do you do a pre-emergent, or mostly post-emergent for weeds? |
| 9 | Homeowner | Post... I guess? I just spray the weeds when they pop up. |
| 10 | Rep | Makes sense. Mind if I show you something interesting on your lawn? I promise I'm not here to hard sell you. |
| 11 | Homeowner | Sure, I'm Steve by the way. |
| 12 | Rep | Good to meet you, Steve. See this area here where the grass is patchy? |
| 13 | Homeowner | Yeah, I've been trying to thicken that up. |
| 14 | Rep | The challenge is timing. This is where crabgrass took over last summer, right? |
| 15 | Homeowner | How'd you know? |
| 16 | Rep | I can see the ghost pattern. Without a pre-emergent in early spring, crabgrass germinates before you even notice it. Then you're chasing it all summer. |
| 17 | Homeowner | I did fight crabgrass all year. Thought I just missed spots with the weed killer. |
| 18 | Rep | Common issue. Pre-emergent creates a barrier before germination. Post-emergent only kills what's already growing. Two different battles. |
| 19 | Homeowner | They don't really explain that at Lowe's. |
| 20 | Rep | No, they don't. And the timing window for pre-emergent is about 2 weeks. Miss it, and you're fighting weeds all season. How much time do you spend on your lawn each month? |
| 21 | Homeowner | I don't know... couple hours a month during season? Maybe 15-20 hours total. |
| 22 | Rep | And if I told you I could get you better results, with products you can't buy retail, for about the same cost? |
| 23 | Homeowner | I'd say prove it. |
| 24 | Rep | Fair. Our annual program is $499 for your lawn size. That's 8 professional applications. |
| 25 | Homeowner | That's more than I spend on products. |
| 26 | Rep | Maybe by $100-150. But our products are commercial grade - they last longer and work better. And you get those 20 hours back. |
| 27 | Homeowner | What about the satisfaction of doing it myself? |
| 28 | Rep | You still mow, edge, do your landscaping. That's the visible stuff. We just handle the chemistry that's honestly harder to get right without training. |
| 29 | Homeowner | The chemistry is where I struggle. I never know the right timing. |
| 30 | Rep | Exactly. That's what we're trained for - soil temps, growth cycles, weather timing. You focus on making it look sharp, we handle the science. |
| 31 | Homeowner | Alright, I'm listening. What's included? |
| 32 | Rep | 8 treatments: early spring pre-emergent, two fertilizer rounds, weed control through summer, grub prevention, fall winterizer. All guaranteed. |
| 33 | Homeowner | And if I'm not happy? |
| 34 | Rep | We retreat until you are. No extra charge. If we can't fix it, you get your money back. |
| 35 | Homeowner | That's better than the hardware store's return policy. |
| 36 | Rep | Right? And if you prepay today, I'll knock off 10%. Makes it $449 for the year. |
| 37 | Homeowner | $449 for the year, and I get my weekends back? |
| 38 | Rep | Your treatment weekends, yeah. You still get to be out here doing the stuff you actually enjoy. |
| 39 | Homeowner | You know what? Let's do it. My wife's been telling me to stop being stubborn. |
| 40 | Rep | Smart man. Let me get you set up. Your lawn's going to look even better, and you'll wonder why you didn't do this sooner. |
| 41 | Narrator | By validating the DIY identity, showing true costs, and positioning partnership over replacement, Marcus converted the DIY warrior to a prepay customer. |
