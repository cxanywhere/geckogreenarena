Title: RP1 (Phase 1): “I’m actually really busy right now.”
Subtitle: Chapter 1 → Opening → First 10 Seconds

Pass Criteria (What Rep Must Do)

Acknowledge time pressure

Set a ≤ 60-second container

Ask a qualifying control question

Fail Conditions

Pitches immediately or disengages

Winning Response (Control-Explicit, Full)
“Totally understand — I’ll be quick. Give me 60 seconds, and if this doesn’t apply I’ll head out. Q›uick question: are you the one who usually handles the lawn here?”

Losing Response (Loss of Control)
“This won’t take long… we do fertilization and weed control.”

How to Succeed

Keep tone respectful and matter-of-fact

Ask the control question before pitching

Move immediately to something specific you observed on the lawn

Slide 8 — ARENA AI Roleplay #2

Title: RP2 (Phase 1): “Not interested.”
Subtitle: Chapter 1 → Opening → Handling Brush-Offs

Pass Criteria (What Rep Must Do)

Acknowledge

Reframe to a specific issue

Redirect with a control question

Fail Conditions

Argues or accepts rejection immediately

Winning Response (Control-Explicit, Full)
“That’s fair — most people weren’t interested either until I pointed out one thing. Are you seeing weeds along the curb here, or is that already handled?”

Losing Response (Loss of Control)
“Okay, no worries.”

Do not debate

Do not “sell” yet—diagnose

Use a visible reference (curb line, edge, thin area)

Slide 19 — Why Some Weeds Keep Coming Back

Title: WHY SOME WEEDS KEEP COMING BACK
Subtitle: The seed bank explains ongoing weed pressure

Annual weeds

One season lifecycle

Drop seeds → new weeds later

Perennial weeds

Return from roots year after year

Harder to fully eliminate

The seed bank (simple explanation)

Soil contains dormant weed seeds that can activate over time

Weather, watering, and disturbance can trigger growth

Even after killing weeds, new ones can emerge

Key message: We focus on control and reduction over time, not instant perfection.

Slide 20 — Pre-Emergent vs Post-Emergent

Title: PRE-EMERGENT VS. POST-EMERGENT
Subtitle: Timing is everything

Pre-emergent (Prevention)

Stops weeds before they sprout

Creates a barrier that interrupts seed development

Reduces future weeds

Post-emergent (Treatment)

Targets existing weeds

Visible results typically in 10–14 days (varies)
