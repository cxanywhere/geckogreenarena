Slide — Foundations: Tracking, Measurement, and Feed Readiness

1) Conversion measurement that supports ROAS decisions

Confirm primary conversion events (purchase, checkout start, add-to-cart) and ensure correct revenue capture.

Validate event integrity before scaling budgets.

2) Product feed integrity (Google Merchant Center + Microsoft Merchant Center)

Audit feed attributes and disapprovals; fix root causes.

Maintain accurate price and availability (these are core Merchant Center attributes).

Enforce product identifiers (e.g., GTIN) where required; missing identifiers can reduce eligibility for Shopping features.

3) Microsoft Shopping readiness

Stand up Microsoft Merchant Center store and import/sync feeds from Google where appropriate (Microsoft supports feed import from Google Ads).

Ensure conversion tracking readiness (UET and conversion goals are key prerequisites for Smart Shopping style setups).

How we do it

Governance

Slide — Landing Pages: CRO & A/B Testing (Examples)

Goal

Landing page testing approach

Examples:

Slide — Arena AI Enablement: What We Deliver in Phase 1

Objective
Create a repeatable, measurable certification system that improves real-world performance through practice, feedback, and accountability—not just content consumption.

Why roleplay is core
Roleplay is an “active practice” modality associated with much higher retention than lecture-style training per Association for Talent Development.

Phase 1 deliverables

Blendtec Sales Certification Programs (B2C + B2B/Commercial) for:

Blendtec internal reps

Partners (dealers/distributors/resellers)

Complete training assets: micro-lessons, playbooks, call flows, objection handling

AI roleplays + scoring rubrics + pass/fail criteria

Quizzes + knowledge checks + recertification cadence

Manager dashboards + coaching workflows (who needs help, what to coach next)

Slide — Certification Architecture (Who Gets Certified and On What)

Track 1: B2C Sales Certification (Retail/DTC)

Product knowledge: key models, accessories, positioning, “why Blendtec”

Consumer discovery: use cases (smoothies, soups, family, fitness, gifting)

Objections: price, “too powerful,” noise, cleaning, counter space, warranty

Conversion behaviors: guided selection, bundling, urgency/offer framing (as allowed)

Track 2: B2B/Commercial Certification (Lead-to-Opportunity)

Commercial discovery: volume, duty cycle, menu, environment, service expectations

Solution mapping: model fit, containers, use cases, ROI language (without over-claiming)

“Where to buy” routing: e-tailers and distributor pathways; compliance language

Track 3: Partner Enablement Certification (Dealers/Distributors/Resellers)

Brand standards and messaging consistency

Channel compliance and “authorized selling” norms

Handling competitive comparisons and procurement questions

Escalation and lead handoff rules

Certification levels

Level 1: Product + basics (knowledge + simple scenarios)

Level 2: Objections + scenario mastery (roleplays)

Level 3: Advanced/Commercial (deal complexity, procurement, multi-stakeholder)

Slide — Training Content We Create (Curriculum + Assets)

Curriculum format (microlearning + spaced reinforcement)

Short modules designed to be consumed quickly and revisited frequently

Reinforcement loops: “learn → quiz → roleplay → feedback → retry”
Microlearning and spaced reinforcement are widely used to support retention and ongoing skill development.

Assets we produce

“Blendtec Sales Playbook” (messaging pillars, talk tracks, do/don’t claims)

Product one-pagers (by model) + comparison sheets

Discovery question banks (B2C and Commercial)

Objection handling library (scripts + principles)

Partner-ready collateral: “how to sell,” “how to demo,” “how to route leads”

Knowledge checks

Quizzes after each module (5–10 questions)

Scenario-based questions (not just recall)

Slide — AI Roleplays: Scenario Library, Realism, and Skill Transfer

Roleplay design principles

Scenarios replicate real pressure: time constraints, skepticism, competitive mentions

Progressive difficulty: beginner → intermediate → advanced

Personalities: rushed buyer, skeptical buyer, price-focused buyer, procurement-driven buyer

Roleplay categories (examples)

B2C:

“I can get a cheaper blender on Amazon.”

“I don’t want something hard to clean.”

“Which model is best for smoothies + nut butters?”

Commercial:

“We need reliability for peak hours—what’s the duty cycle?”

“We buy through approved vendors—how do we source?”

“We’re evaluating multiple brands—why should we switch?”

Partner:

“Customer wants your price matched.”

“How do we stay consistent with Blendtec messaging?”

“How do we handle warranty/service expectations?”

Why this works

Roleplay and active practice are repeatedly emphasized as high-value for skill transfer in enablement.

Slide — Scoring, Pass/Fail Criteria, and Certification Rules

Each roleplay has a rubric (example dimensions)

Discovery quality (asks the right questions)

Accuracy (product claims and fit)

Objection handling technique (acknowledge → clarify → respond → confirm)

Clarity and confidence (structure, tone, next step)

Compliance (channel-safe promises and routing language)

Pass/Fail structure

Must-hit “non-negotiables” (e.g., wrong claim = auto-fail)

Score thresholds (e.g., 80% overall + 100% on compliance items)

Retake logic: targeted remediation modules assigned automatically

Certification rules

Level 1: pass modules + quizzes + 3 roleplays

Level 2: pass 6–10 roleplays + objection mastery threshold

Level 3: commercial scenarios + procurement + handoff flows

Training evaluation framework
We will report outcomes using a training evaluation approach aligned to the 4 levels (reaction, learning, behavior, results) described by Kirkpatrick Partners.
