Slide 16 — Setting Expectations: The Partnership Agreement

Title: The Partnership Agreement
Subtitle: Clear expectations = happy customers

Slide 21 — B-P-D-C: Your Objection Compass

Title: B-P-D-C — The Universal Framework

B — Buffer
Acknowledge and diffuse: “I understand.”

P — Probe
Ask to uncover the root: “What worries you?”

D — Discover
Identify the true concern (what’s really stopping them)

C — Close
Present a tailored solution and confidently move forward

Key instruction
You do not “answer objections.” You discover them, then close.

Slide 23 — Objection Examples: Quick Responses (Framework-Based)

Title: Quick Responses
Subtitle: Use B-P-D-C in real language

For “Not interested”

Buffer: “Totally understand.”

Probe: “Just to be sure I’m not missing something—anything specific you’re trying to improve?”

Discover: Identify the issue they care about

Close: “Would you be open to a quick look so I can show you what I’m seeing?”

For “DIY / Existing Company”

Respect: “That’s great—it shows you care about your lawn.”

Probe: “What’s working well, and what would you like improved?”

Discover: Isolate the gap (edge weeds, thinning, color)

Close: “Can we do a quick 2-minute walk so I can point out what I’d address?”

Slide 37 — Final Certification Assessment (Post-Training)

Title: RP16 (All Phases): Final Certification Assessment
Subtitle: Rep must show control across the full flow

Scenario design (assessment rules)

The homeowner triggers objections across ≥3 phases:

Phase 1: “I’m busy” or “Not interested”

Phase 2/3: “We already have a lawn company” OR “My lawn looks fine” OR “I don’t see the issue”

Phase 5 (mandatory): “Why do I have to pay now?” OR “I need to think about it” OR “I’ll call you”

Pass Criteria

Rep maintains control across ≥3 phases

Rep successfully handles a Phase 5 close objection

Rep stays in discovery and advances toward a decision

Fail Conditions

Avoids payment objection

Concedes the decision (“call me whenever”)

Loses control at any phase (pitches early, argues, or exits without resolution)

Success definition (what “pass” looks like)

Acknowledges → directs next step → stays calm → closes with confidence
