A) Sales Fundamentals (8 roleplays)

F1) Cold Call Opener (Permission + Relevance)

Persona: VP Digital / Head of Ecommerce

Goal: Earn 60 seconds and book discovery.

Pass: Clear “why you / why now” in <30 seconds + direct ask.

F2) First Meeting Agenda Control (Avoid “feature tour”)

Scenario: Buyer wants a demo immediately.

Goal: Secure discovery-first agenda and success criteria.

Pass: Buyer agrees to a structured agenda (needs → KPIs → demo).

F3) SPIN Discovery Drill (Situation → Problem → Implication → Need-payoff)

Goal: Build consultative questioning flow.

Pass: Buyer states business impact and desired outcome in their own words.

F4) MEDDIC Qualification Check (Metrics, Economic Buyer, Decision Criteria/Process, Pain, Champion)

Goal: Qualify enterprise deals rigorously.

Pass: Rep identifies missing MEDDIC fields and assigns next actions.

F5) Challenger “Teach–Tailor–Take Control” Commercial Teaching

Goal: Reframe status quo and create constructive urgency.

Pass: Buyer acknowledges risk of inaction + agrees to next step.

F6) Objection Handling Ladder (Acknowledge → Clarify → Reframe → Evidence → Confirm)

Objections: “We already have a tool,” “IT won’t allow,” “Not priority.”

Pass: Objection reduces, and decision path becomes clearer.

F7) Pricing & Procurement (Value framing + Paper Process)

Goal: Prevent discount-first negotiation; navigate procurement steps.

Pass: Rep ties pricing to outcomes and confirms paper process owners (MEDDPICC-style).

F8) Close with Mutual Action Plan (MAP)

Goal: Convert interest into dated plan (stakeholders, security, pilot, decision).

Pass: Confirmed timeline + meeting invites + owners.

B) Monetate Products & Solutions (10 roleplays)

P1) Platform Reposition: “Why unify experience optimization now?”

Goal: Shift conversation from point tools → unified platform (AI + personalization + experimentation).

Pass: Agreement to workshop use cases across suites.

P2) Symphony (Personalization) – 1:1 experiences at scale

Persona: VP Ecommerce / Head of Digital Experience

Pass: Defined 2–3 personalization journeys + measurement plan.

P3) Maestro (Experimentation) – enterprise-grade experimentation across layers

Persona: CRO Lead / Experimentation Director / Product

Pass: Testing governance + first 90-day experiment backlog.

P4) Overture (Search & Discovery) – intent + context beyond keyword search

Persona: Ecommerce Product / Merchandising

Pass: Search audit + “zero-results / conversion” baseline + pilot scope.

P5) Concierge (Services) – “software + expert execution”

Persona: Lean team needing acceleration

Pass: Clear services engagement scope (enablement vs managed execution).

P6) A/B/n Testing – “zero-flicker, omnichannel reach”

Persona: Growth + Engineering

Pass: Agreement on testing locations + guardrails + rollout plan.

P7) Feature Experimentation & Rollouts – “de-risk releases”

Persona: Product + Engineering

Pass: First feature flag test plan + rollback criteria.

P8) Dynamic Testing – AI-optimized allocation to reduce time-to-value

Persona: CRO / Experimentation

Pass: Select 1 journey for dynamic testing + ROI measurement.

P9) Dynamic Bundles – “increase AOV without blanket discounting”

Persona: Merchandising + Growth

Pass: Bundle hypothesis + segments + AOV target and guardrails.

P10) “Suite-to-Roadmap” Packaging Roleplay (Symphony + Maestro + Overture + Concierge)

Scenario: Buyer is unsure what to start with.

Pass: Rep proposes a phased adoption roadmap aligned to outcomes.

C) Verticals & Industry Use Cases (6 roleplays)

Monetate highlights sector-specific value across Commerce & Retail, Travel/Hospitality/Leisure, Financial Services & Insurance, Healthcare & Regulated, and Communications/Media/Technology.

V1) Commerce & Retail – “optimize every cart”

Focus: conversion, basket size, loyalty; combine testing + personalization.

Pass: 90-day roadmap: 3 experiments + 2 personalization launches.

V2) Travel & Hospitality – “intent-driven journeys that lift bookings + loyalty”

Pass: 2 booking-flow experiments + 1 loyalty personalization use case.

V3) Financial Services & Insurance – “trust + compliance while reducing friction”

Pass: Approved pilot path + compliant data boundaries + security stakeholder alignment.

V4) Healthcare & Regulated – “secure, compliant personalization that reduces costs”

Pass: One portal-flow test + one compliant personalization use case.

V5) Communications, Media & Technology – “engagement and retention”

Pass: Trial-to-paid experiment plan + retention personalization test.

V6) Vertical Curveball – “multi-brand / multi-region enterprise complexity”

Scenario: Global org, multiple sites, competing teams, tool sprawl.

Pass: Governance model + phased rollout by region/brand + MAP.
